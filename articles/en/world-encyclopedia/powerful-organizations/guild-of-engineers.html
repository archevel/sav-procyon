Guild of Engineers

One of the Hegemonic High Guilds, responsible for resource acquisition, cybernetics, AI tech advancement, and research and development. Decadent, powerful, and utterly ruthless.

Structure

The Guild is set up like a corporation, with Chief Executive Avalon Riat as the unquestioned leader. He maintains various Heads in what we would think of as VP levels, including Operations, Finance, Research, etc.

Culture

The Guild is a cut-throat organization, dedicated to ruthless exploitation of the Sector's workers and resources, and furthering practical knowledge of Precursor artifacts, cybernetics, AI, Urbots, and more. Promotions go to both the brilliant and the underhanded, and those who fit both categories are the ones who ascend to become Heads.
