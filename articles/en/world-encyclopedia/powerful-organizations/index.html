Powerful Organizations

The Kingdoms, Countries, Guilds and Organized Crime in Procyon Sector IE-21

Contents

  • Cobalt Syndicate — Once a labor union, the Cobalt Syndicate has turned to smuggling and extortion to carve out shipping lanes of their own and have a real say in sector economics
  • Cult of the Seekers — Wandering mystics seeking artifacts and exploring, with an eye toward expanding the Hegemony to the furthest reaches of space.
  • Guild of Engineers — One of the Hegemonic High Guilds, responsible for resource acquisition, cybernetics, AI tech advancement, and research and development.
  • Turner Society — The Turner Society is a loose-knit organization of society houses and high-end clubs throughout the Procyon Sector.
