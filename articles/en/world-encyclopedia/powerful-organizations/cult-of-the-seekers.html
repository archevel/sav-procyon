Cult of the Seekers

Wandering mystics seeking artifacts and exploring, with an eye toward expanding the Hegemony to the furthest reaches of space. The current Hegemon's mother is a member.

Public Agenda

  • To expand the rule of the Hegemony.
  • To support the Hegemon and Hegemonic Law.
  • To open the Hantu Gate and to stabilize others.
  • To acquire Precursor artifacts to aid in these efforts.

Assets

  • Research station on a small island on Mem, near the Hantu Gate.
  • An archaeological dig site on Shimaya.
  • Extensive Guild schematics of Gates.
  • Hegemonic sanction to retrieve and study artifacts.
  • A large stockpile of artifacts.

Tenets of Faith

  • It is a sacred duty to expand the Hegemony and the rule of law across the universe.
  • The secrets of the Precursors will lead us to ultimate truth.

Ethics

  • Any deed which furthers the reach of the Hegemony is permitted.
  • So long as it does not conflict with our ambition, the Hegemonic Law should be considered sacred.

Political Influence & Intrigue

The Cult of the Seekers was a heretic and outcast cult until the Ascension Wars completed. As the current Hegemon's mother is a prominent member of the Cult, it has become one of the more politically powerful officially sanctioned cults.
