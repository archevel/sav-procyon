Turner Society

The Turner Society is a loose-knit organization of society houses and high-end clubs throughout the Procyon Sector.

Membership is both exclusive and expensive, and considered a mark of exquisite taste by members of high society and Noble Houses.

Public Agenda

The Turner Society allows the rich and well-connected places to relax, retreat, and network away from the prying eyes of newsmen and bureaucrats.
