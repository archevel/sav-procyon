Noble Houses

The galaxy is carved into sectors, put under the stewardship of seven Noble Houses.

The Legion and their battle cruisers are military, and swear fealty to the Hegemon, but answer to the Noble House that rules the system.

They can be called in to handle matters that require extra force or have proven too complex for the System Police to handle.

Large-scale action from the Core tends to be slow, be it from the Hegemon proper or House Head.
