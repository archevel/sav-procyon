Cobalt Syndicate

Once a labor union, the Cobalt Syndicate has turned to smuggling and extortion to carve out shipping lanes of their own and have a real say in sector economics and politics.

Structure

Boss Jax is the current Boss of the Syndicate.

His Captains man, pilot, and oversee the shipping and smuggling operations.

His Lieutenants oversee ground-based operations.

Public Agenda

  • To support and solidify the power of organized labor across the Procyon Sector.
  • To level the playing field between nobles, magnates, and everyday workers.
  • To retaliate against transgressions by any means necessary.

Assets

  • A fleet of private ships (often used for illicit operations.)
  • Toughs, thugs, and laborers on nearly every planet, port, and station in the sector.
  • A major berth and associated docks on Warren.

Type

Illicit, Syndicate

Leader

Jax

Notable Members

  • Jet Curia
  • Sephua
