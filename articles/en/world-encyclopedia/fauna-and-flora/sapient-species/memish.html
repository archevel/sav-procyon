Memish

The Memish peoples are a deep-sea aquatic race native to the planet Mem in the Holt System. Consequently the Memish are not an unfamiliar sight in the Procyon sector.

Although they’re generally humanoid, the Memish have pitch-black eyes, and skin that comes in various shades of blue, purple, and green. Instead of hair they have tentacles. The Memish are able to survive at extreme depths, and navigate waters with virtually no light available.

They’re known for organizing in complex, extended family groups, and practicing their religion away from the light of the Holt star.

Basic Information

Anatomy

The Memish are hairless, scaled bipedal amphibians, bearing both lungs and gills. The lungs can be filled with water to avoid pressurization issues in the deep oceans. They are, as individuals, far stronger than a human, and possess retractable claws capable of filleting sharkskin. However, they can be slow and clumsy on land.

Ecology and Habitats

The Memish are a benthonic race and best suited to extremely cold, high-pressure environments. They do not tolerate extreme heat or aridity at all well and can even be killed outright by exposure to temperatures well within human endurance.

Additional Information

Social Structure

The Memish organize in complex, extended family groups. The Memish word for family encompasses a bewildering number of relationships.

Perception and Sensory Capabilities

Memish senses are extraordinary. They are capable of hearing exceedingly low frequencies, can navigate flawlessly even in pitch blackness, and have shown capabilities of sensing gravitic disturbances, which are a common issue on their homeworld.

Civilization and Culture

Naming Traditions

Human names and surnames are taken in order to communicate between species. Amongst themselves the Memish use both sub-vocal and low-grade electrical pulses to communicate.

Beauty Ideals

Most adult Memish are covered in scars, which follow patterns based on family structure, religious practices, and personal preferences. Several Memish have packed these scars with deep-ocean bioluminescence, which may or may not be a standard of beauty - few humans have bothered to ask.

History

Human awareness of the Memish people's history is limited. The human colonies on Mem were in place for over one hundred years before the two species met, and the meeting was acrimonious, quickly escalating into all-out war.

Hegemonic forces broke the native military in a three-year war, and has since incorporated them more or less successfully into the Hegemony.

Interspecies Relations and Assumptions

The Memish are by and large a part of the Hegemony today, though rumors abound that there are entire family units still below the surface and unaware that they are a conquered people.
