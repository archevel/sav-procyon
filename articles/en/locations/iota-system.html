Iota System

A twin star system, Iota is a mixed bag. Though many people only pass through it ojn the way to Brekk, Iota is the industrial production house that transforms many of the resources of Rin System and Iota into usable goods for the rest of the sector, and the Hegemony.

The sector's Starsmiths shipyards are here, meaning that Iota usually has a significant Legion presence. Amerath is a popular vacation spot for well-off families, seeming a bit rustic and rural with "charming" ruins, while also sporting enough control and tech to support luxury.
