Vos

Known throughout Procyon by its nickname "Glimmer," the surface of this enormous planet is made of carbon compounuds such as graphite and diamond. At night, the largest crystal formations glow with unearthly light - a property many of the crystals retain after being cut. One must dock at Guild Station IA-23 in orbit, and shuttle down to do any business on the surface.

People and Places:

  • Guild Station IA-23: The only legal port on Vos, heavily run and monitored by the Guild.
  • Impera Evazan: High-ranking Guild logistics officer responsible for crystal mining, with in-depth knowledge of much of the Guild's supply chain structure.
  • Morek and RA-NA: The most feared bounty hunter in Procyon and his AI partner. On retainer to hunt down anyone who crosses the Guild.
  • Yola Sprekk: Jeweler known for using the unique properties of Vos crystals. Her creations may be the most artful pieces in Procyon. A Sprekk piece can open doors to the most elite circles.

RULES: Vosian crystals are prized by Guilds and mystics alike. Vos is full of money, but closely monitored. When you run a job on Vos, you gain +1 Cred and +1 Heat.
