Holt System

Holt was the second system in Procyon to be colonized, though the Rin-Holt Gate was difficult to stabilize. It had to be forced open, but only wild theories abound as to why the Precursors may have wanted it shut in the first place - nor why or how the large, never-opened Hantu Gate that may lead to further systems has been sealed.

The Holt Star burns white, though it is far older than stars of this type should be, which scientists attribute to ancient Ur manipulation.

It has xenos unhappy about the yoke of the Hegemony, criminal and pirate strongholds, and one of the richest (and most fiercely guarded) Guild mining planets. In a sector known for its ragtag populace, Holt is the biggest hotbed of scum and villainy.

--- Gates

  • The Rin-Holt Gate is known to be unstable at best.
  • The Hantu Gate has never been successfully opened.

--- Planets, Ports, and Places

THE HANTU GATE: The Hegemony has never been able to activate this Gate. Compared to the operational gates, it appears to be missing some small but critical components. It is speculated that the Precursors locked the gate and hid the keys, though it's anyone's guess as to why ...

JEREC'S JUNKYARD: A free-floating mass of ships and parts, connected by cables and magnetism. If you're looking for equipment on the cheap, the Junkyard is your place, though it's likely missing pieces or any reliability. Jerec also buys ... but he is a canny haggler.

MEM : This ocean planet was colonized by the Hegemony for almost one hundred years before deep-water aquatic xenos made themselves (and their planetary claims) known. Hegemonic forces broke the Memish military and incorporated them into the Hegemony. Exploration of Mem has proven difficult due to the free-standing gravity wells deep beneath the waves.

PLANET OMEGA: Three survey crews and one military expedition have vanished on Omega before the Hegemony quarantined this planet. It's overrun with a deadly lifeform that nests in Ur ruins and can resist nukes from orbit.

SONHANDRA : This planet is tidally locked - the same side of the planet faces the star at all times. The Dayside is blistering, and, oddly, all light sources extinguish themselves about one kilometer into the Nightside. Most of the settlements are along the twilit border zone, including the capital of Ugar. Known for lax policies regarding trade, it's a choice destination for smugglers and fences alike

TRADE PLATFORM AUTO #04: The Guild has set up an automated platform for selling fuel, covered in defense mechanisms to prevent outright thefts. Because of this, some parties conduct negotiations here to discourage escalation.

VOS : Known throughout Procyon by its nickname "Glimmer," the surface of this enormous planet is made of carbon compounuds such as graphite and diamond. At night, the largest crystal formations glow with unearthly light - a property many of the crystals retain after being cut. One must dock at Guild Station IA-23 in orbit, and shuttle down to do any business on the surface.
