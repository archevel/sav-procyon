Todav and Dendara

Todav is the fifth moon of Nightfall. On its surface sits Dendara — an ancient temple. Some say it's an Ur temple, others that it's the remains of a forgotten mystic Cult. Its derelict corridors are tough to tour due to the moon's lack of atmosphere and the glitching effect it has on drives and electronics.
