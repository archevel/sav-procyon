Brekk System

The cultural capital of Procyon. If you're looking to file paperwork, strike deals, and mingle with the elite of the sector, you'll find many of them on the neon-lit streets of Nightfall. And if you're looking to do research, there's no better place than Khalud Academy on Shimaya.

Brekk also supports an untamed, largely unexplored jungle world that can hide those fleeing from the law, and entice the rich on hunts for dangerous game.
