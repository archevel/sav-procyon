Ashtari Cloud

An ancient damaged remnant of a massive Ur-ship lies in space, generating an in-system nebula. Within the Cloud, normal propulsion is limited and nav systems are dodgy. Rumor states that the pirate fleet known as Maelstrom  has learned how to navigate through the Cloud, and have made it their base of operations. The frequency of Maelstrom attacks in the Rin System lend credence to this theory, though both the Guild of Engineers and the Hegemonic Cults insist that it could not be done without Precursor technology or a blatant misuse of The Way.
