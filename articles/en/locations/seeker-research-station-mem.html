Seeker Research Station, Mem

The Cult of the Seekers have long maintained a research base here, even prior to the Ascension Wars, in order to study the Hantu Gate more closely - as well as to monitor the activities of other organizations which might have an interest in this mystery.

Purpose / Function

To further research and hopefully open the Hantu Gate, locked in orbit around Mem but inaccessible to the Hegemony at this time.

Alterations

After making contact with and subjugating the Mem, the Seekers realized they may be valuable allies. An aquatic center, airlock, and decompression chamber have been added onto the original station.

Architecture

Domes of heavily tempered glass and high-tech plastics.

Type

Laboratory

Parent Location

Mem

Owning Organization

Cult of the Seekers
