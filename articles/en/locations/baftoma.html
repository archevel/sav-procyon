Baftoma

AKA "The Husk." Resource exploitation by the Hegemony is comprehensive, and planets incapable of sustaining life are stripped to the core. Baftoma is a prime example - only scaffolding of rock and left-behind broken-down equipment remains, its shattered form only used by spacers in hiding or dodging heat.
