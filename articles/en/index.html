Procyon Sector IE-21

A Wretched Hive of Scum and Villainy ...

The sector is a burgeoning place for criminal factions, syndicates, mystics, and other folks who live on the fringes. Too far and too small to have the strong arm of the Hegemony truly clutched around its throat, Procyon is also a hotbed of corruption, peppered with strange temples and Ur artifacts that haven't yet been picked completely clean. It provides homes for those that may be fleeing the burning light of civilization that emanates from the Core, and opportunities for those who thrive in the darkness.
