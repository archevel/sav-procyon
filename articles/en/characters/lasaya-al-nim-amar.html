Lady Lasaya al'Nim-Amar

The current head of the Cult of the Seekers.

Lady Lasaya is both a brilliant researcher and a passionate mystic. She acknowledges that there are things in the Way which science does not yet understand, and that ignoring such things can lead to overlooking vital information.
