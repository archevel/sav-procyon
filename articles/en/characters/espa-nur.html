Espa Nur

A massive, burly Memish labor boss, his many scars packed tight with deep-ocean bioluminescence.

He intimidated the Deep Blues into dropping off smuggled weapons without payment, and left a permanent dimple on the left side of Xeta Fforde's face as a reminder of the encounter.
