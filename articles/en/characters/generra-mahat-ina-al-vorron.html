Lady Generra Mahat ina-al'Vorron

The Deep Blues encountered Lady Generra at a Cobalt Syndicate dock on Warren, in the midst of booking passage for herself and a pair of studious-looking twins with their extraction target, Jet Curia.

She wears the insignia of both the Cult of the Seekers and the muted colors signifying a branch of the Noble House Vorron.

After a botched negotiation, she was briefly taken hostage by Jet and was shot - by him - in the resulting scene. Ace attempted to perform battlefield surgery but was unsuccessful. Xeta's quick action took Jet out and, after negotiations between Boxcar and Sephua, the Deep Blues left with herself, Ignis Bartok and Malthus Bartok.

Ace was able to tend to her on board the ship, at which point she contracted for passage to the Seeker Research Station on Mem.
